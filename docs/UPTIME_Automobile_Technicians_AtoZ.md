# UPTIME — Automobile Mechanics A to Z
## All Vehicle Mechanics from Basic to Advanced

> **Scope:** 2-Wheeler → Car → Truck → EV → Heavy Vehicle
> **DSATM Bengaluru — VTU — 2026-27**

---

### A
- **AC Mechanic (Auto)** — fixes car AC, compressor, condenser, gas charging (R134a/R1234yf), blower motor
- **Auto Electrician** — fixes car wiring, battery, alternator, fuses, sensors, ECU wiring harness
- **Auto Body Repair Technician** — fixes dents, panel beating, accident damage repair
- **Alignment & Balancing Technician** — wheel alignment (4-wheel), tyre balancing, tracking
- **Auto Glass Technician** — windshield replacement, power window repair, defogger repair
- **ADAS Technician** — fixes Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (new role 2024+), cameras, radar sensors
- **Auto Upholstery Technician** — fixes car seats, dashboard, interior fabrics, carpets

### B
- **Battery Technician (Auto)** — fixes car batteries, jump start, battery load testing, replacement
- **Body Shop Painter** — car painting, dent removal + painting, colour matching, clear coat
- **Brake Mechanic** — fixes disc brakes, drum brakes, brake pads, callipers, brake fluid
- **Bike Mechanic (2-Wheeler)** — fixes motorcycles, scooters, engine, gear box, chain drive
- **Bearing Replacement Technician** — wheel bearings, hub bearings on cars and trucks

### C
- **Car Mechanic (General)** — overall car repair — engine, gearbox, suspension, brakes
- **CNG/LPG Kit Technician** — installs/repairs CNG kits, LPG conversion, gas valves, regulators
- **Clutch Specialist** — clutch plate, pressure plate, release bearing, flywheel replacement
- **Coolant System Technician** — radiator repair, coolant flush, thermostat, water pump
- **Carburettor Mechanic** — old vehicles, carburettor cleaning, needle valve, jet cleaning
- **Commercial Vehicle Mechanic** — trucks, buses, tempo travellers, heavy vehicle repair
- **Crane Mounted Vehicle Mechanic** — boom trucks, crane vehicles, hydraulic boom systems

### D
- **Diesel Mechanic (Vehicles)** — diesel cars, trucks, buses, fuel injection, diesel pumps
- **Differential Mechanic** — rear differential, front differential, diff oil change, pinion gear
- **Diagnostics Technician** — OBD scanner, fault code reading, ECU diagnostics, DPF issues
- **Denting & Painting Technician** — panel beating, putty work, primer, painting, finishing

### E
- **Engine Overhaul Mechanic** — full engine rebuild, pistons, rings, valves, crankshaft
- **Engine Tuning Technician** — ECU tuning, performance upgrades, fuel map adjustments
- **Exhaust System Technician** — exhaust pipe, catalytic converter, DPF, silencer repair
- **EV Technician** — electric vehicle battery, motor, inverter, BMS, charging system repair
- **EV Battery Specialist** — high-voltage battery pack, cell replacement, BMS diagnostics
- **EV Charging Technician** — EV charging stations, AC/DC chargers, EVSE installation
- **Electronic Fuel Injection (EFI) Technician** — fuel injectors, fuel rail, MAP sensor, ECU

### F
- **Fuel Injection Specialist** — common rail diesel, petrol direct injection, injector cleaning
- **Fuel System Mechanic** — fuel pump, fuel filter, fuel tank, fuel lines repair
- **Fleet Maintenance Technician** — maintains company fleets (cars, vans, trucks) — scheduled service
- **Four Wheeler Mechanic** — cars, SUVs, MUVs, jeeps — all mechanical repair

### G
- **Gearbox Mechanic** — manual gearbox repair, synchromesh, gear shift issues
- **General Service Technician** — routine service — oil change, filters, spark plugs, fluids

### H
- **Heavy Vehicle Mechanic** — trucks (6/10/12/16 wheeler), buses, mining vehicles
- **Hybrid Vehicle Technician** — hybrid battery, dual powertrain, regenerative braking
- **Hydraulic System Mechanic** — truck hydraulic lifts, tipper hydraulics, power steering
- **Horn & Electrical Accessories Mechanic** — horns, music systems, lights, accessories fitting

### I
- **Infotainment Technician** — car audio, touchscreen, GPS navigation, Bluetooth systems
- **Injector Cleaning Technician** — ultrasonic fuel injector cleaning, flow testing

### J
- **Jeep / 4x4 Specialist** — off-road vehicles, 4WD transfer case, differential locks, lift kits

### K
- **Knuckle & Steering Repair Technician** — steering knuckle, steering rack, tie rod, ball joint

### L
- **LCV Mechanic (Light Commercial Vehicle)** — Tata Ace, Ashok Leyland DOST, Eicher trucks
- **Leaf Spring / Suspension Mechanic** — suspension springs, shock absorbers, struts, bushes
- **Lighting Technician (Auto)** — headlights, LED upgrades, DRL, taillight, interior lights

### M
- **Multi-Brand Car Mechanic** — works on all brands (not brand-specific)
- **Motor Cycle Mechanic** — bikes (Hero, Bajaj, Royal Enfield, Honda, TVS, Yamaha)
- **Maruti / Tata / Hyundai Authorised Technician** — brand-specific trained mechanic

### N
- **NVH Technician (Noise Vibration Harshness)** — diagnoses and fixes rattles, vibrations, squeaks

### O
- **OBD Diagnostics Specialist** — reads fault codes, clears check engine light, ECU programming
- **Off-Road Vehicle Mechanic** — 4WD systems, rock crawlers, safari vehicles

### P
- **Panel Beater** — straightens bent body panels after accident, before painting
- **Power Window / Central Lock Technician** — power windows, central locking, sunroof, mirrors
- **Power Steering Mechanic** — hydraulic power steering, EPS (electric power steering) repair
- **Pre-Delivery Inspection (PDI) Technician** — checks new vehicles before delivery to customer
- **Pre-Purchase Inspection Technician** — checks used cars for buyers (UPTIME unique feature)
- **Petrol Engine Mechanic** — petrol engine repair, timing belt, valve clearance, spark plugs

### Q
- **Quality Control Inspector (Auto)** — inspects repaired vehicles for quality before delivery

### R
- **Radiator Mechanic** — radiator repair, re-coring, flush, cap replacement
- **Recovery Vehicle Mechanic** — tow trucks, cranes, breakdown vans — vehicle recovery equipment
- **Royal Enfield Specialist** — old bullet engines, carburettor, electrical system, classic bikes
- **Roadside Assistance Technician** — emergency repair on road — tyre change, jump start, tow

### S
- **Suspension Specialist** — coilover, shock absorber, ball joint, control arm, bushings
- **Spark Plug & Ignition Technician** — spark plugs, ignition coil, distributor, HT leads
- **Scooter Mechanic** — automatic scooters (Honda Activa, TVS Jupiter, Suzuki Access)
- **Seat Belt & Airbag Technician** — SRS airbag system, seat belt pretensioner, clock spring
- **Sunroof / Panoramic Roof Technician** — sunroof mechanism, seal replacement, drain clearing

### T
- **Tyre Technician** — tyre fitting, balancing, rotation, alignment, tubeless puncture repair
- **Tractor Mechanic** — farm tractors (Mahindra, John Deere, Sonalika) — engine, PTO, hydraulic
- **Truck Mechanic** — heavy trucks — engine overhaul, gearbox, axle, braking systems
- **Transmission Specialist** — automatic gearbox, CVT, DSG, AMT, torque converter repair
- **Timing Belt / Chain Specialist** — timing belt change, timing chain tensioner, VVT system
- **Two Wheeler Mechanic** — bikes and scooters, carburettor, FI, gear box, clutch, battery

### U
- **Underbody Coating Technician** — anti-rust coating, underbody protection, rustproofing
- **Used Car Reconditioning Technician** — prepares used cars for resale — full refurbishment

### V
- **Vehicle Diagnostics Technician** — advanced ECU diagnostics, live data analysis, actuator testing
- **Vehicle Inspection Technician** — fitness certificates, safety checks, RTO inspection prep
- **Vintage / Classic Car Mechanic** — restoration of old vehicles, period-correct repairs

### W
- **Wheel Hub & Bearing Mechanic** — wheel hub assembly, hub bearing, ABS sensor replacement
- **Windshield & Glass Technician** — windshield replacement, ADAS camera recalibration after glass
- **Wiring Harness Technician** — full vehicle wiring, short circuit finding, harness replacement

### X
- **Xenon / HID / LED Headlight Technician** — projector headlight upgrade, HID ballast, LED conversion

### Y
- **Yard / Fleet Inspector** — inspects entire fleet daily, notes issues, schedules service

### Z
- **Zero-Emission Vehicle (ZEV) Specialist** — electric + hydrogen vehicles, future mobility tech
